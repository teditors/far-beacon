An item with affixes found in a region away from home is finer: tier 1 + the distance of that region from home (+1 on the deepest level of the locked place), at most 3. A Fine (2) or Superb (3) item's affixes, curses included, do 50% more per tier above the first. Its fineness shows even before it is studied, and adds 8 coin of value per tier.
